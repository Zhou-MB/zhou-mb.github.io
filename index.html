Mengbing Zhou （周梦兵）

PhD Candidate · University of Amsterdam

Mengbing Zhou is currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Multiscale Networked Systems (MNS) group at the University of Amsterdam (UvA), under the supervision of Prof. Zhiming Zhao and Prof. Adam Belloum. He received his Master's degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), where he worked with Prof. Yang Wang and Prof. Minxian Xu, and his Bachelor's degree from South China University of Technology (SCUT). His research focuses on distributed digital twin systems, multi-agent coordination, and intelligent scheduling in edge-cloud environments.
