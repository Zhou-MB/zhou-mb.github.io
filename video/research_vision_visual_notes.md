# Visual edition — 160 seconds

This edit preserves the original off-white, navy, blue, pale-blue, and warm-accent palette. It keeps the illustrative bus-route story, but separates the explanation into 16 ten-second shots.

## Changes from the 90-second edit

- Remove persistent headlines, shot numbering, and repeated footnotes.
- One short English subtitle per shot; only essential labels inside diagrams.
- Replace text inputs with a bus, rain cloud, sensor signal, and live digital picture.
- Split the opening into motion, rain, and the decision deadline.
- Show route alternatives, a late task, competing jobs, prioritization, agent communication, a broken connection, and re-planning separately.
- Split AI algorithm design into proposing candidates, testing them, and iterating.
- Retain a visual return to the bus and its alternative route.
- Preserve earlier files; the homepage has not been changed.

The film remains silent. Captions are burned into the MP4; a separate VTT transcript is also supplied. Diagrams illustrate a research vision, not measured results. The opening identifies the scenario as illustrative.
