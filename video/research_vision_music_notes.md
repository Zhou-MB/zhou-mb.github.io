# Music edition

The three colon-separated subtitle sentences have been rewritten as complete sentences. The 165-second story, imagery and palette are retained.

The original instrumental score is synthesized locally from sine-based pads and sparse plucked notes, with open chord voicings, light stereo echoes, a four-second fade-in and seven-second fade-out. It uses no sampled recordings, borrowed reference audio or vocals. The final AAC track is normalized to a target of -24 LUFS with a -3 dBTP ceiling to stay in the background.

Files:
- research_vision_music_165s.mp4 — final film with audio
- research_vision_music_165s.vtt — updated English captions
- research_vision_music_source.py — full video render and audio mux
- research_background_music.py — editable original music generator
- research_background_music.wav — standalone score
- research_vision_music_preview.html — comparison player

Run the video source with Python, Pillow, NumPy and imageio-ffmpeg. Earlier versions remain available; the homepage is unchanged.
